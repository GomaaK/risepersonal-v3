What the Snapshot measures

The Snapshot reads five traits from your answers: openness, conscientiousness, extraversion, agreeableness and emotional steadiness. It then matches your pattern to one of twelve brand archetypes and builds your three words from the archetype and your own choices.

Why start here

A personal brand works when it is built on who you are, not on a template. The Snapshot gives you a clear starting point, and it is the first step of how we work with founders, executives and experts across Saudi Arabia, Egypt and the GCC.

Questions

What is the Snapshot?

The Snapshot is a free brand archetype quiz from Rise Personal. It uses twenty statements based on the Big Five personality traits and two choices about how you want to be perceived.

How long does it take?

About four minutes.

Are my answers stored?

No. Your answers and your result stay in your own browser. Nothing is sent to us unless you choose to message Sawaf on WhatsApp.

What do I get?

Your primary archetype, a secondary one, your three words, what to work on, a wellness pairing and a card you can download.

Is it a clinical or hiring test?

No. It is a positioning tool and a starting point for a conversation about your personal brand.
