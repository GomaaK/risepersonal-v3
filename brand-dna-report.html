What the Brand DNA Report measures

The Brand DNA Report reads five traits from your answers: openness, conscientiousness, extraversion, agreeableness and emotional steadiness. It then matches your pattern to one of twelve brand archetypes and builds your three words from the archetype and your own choices.

Why start here

A personal brand works when it is built on who you are, not on a template. The Brand DNA Report gives you a clear starting point, and it is the first step of how we work with founders, executives and experts across Saudi Arabia, Egypt and the GCC.

Questions

What is the Brand DNA Report?

The Brand DNA Report is a free brand archetype quiz from Rise Personal. It uses twenty statements based on the Big Five personality traits and two choices about how you want to be perceived.

How long does it take?

About four minutes.

Are my answers stored?

No. Your answers and your result stay in your own browser. Nothing is sent to us unless you choose to message us on WhatsApp.

What do I get?

A six-page branded PDF: your primary and secondary archetype, your three words, five trait scores, what to work on, a wellness pairing, a personal note from the founder and a seven-day action plan. You also get a card to share.

Is it a clinical or hiring test?

No. It is a positioning tool and a starting point for a conversation about your personal brand.
